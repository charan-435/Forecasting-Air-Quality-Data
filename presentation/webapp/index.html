Methods

What we ran

WaveCatBoost

After Borah et al. (IEEE Sensors Letters, 2025): clean the series, min-max on the pre-test window, Haar MODWT into bands \(D_1,\ldots,D_K,S_K\), fit one CatBoost per band with lag window \(p\), forecast each band, sum to \(\widehat Y\).

Our bake-off

  • Horizons: 1d / 7d / 14d / 31d (24 / 168 / 336 / 744 hours)
  • One chronological origin per horizon; nested windows share the same end
  • Recursive multi-step (not rolling teacher-forced 1-step)
  • MASE with seasonal naive scale \(S=24\)

Paper Table 1

Their table uses Darts-style rolling 1-step forecasts. That is why CPCB PM2.5 7-day WaveCatBoost can look like ~5.07 there and ~1.50 in our recursive table.

Shillong A0–C3

Separate architecture study (direct multi-horizon models). Not a drop-in for the letter WaveCatBoost path; see the presentation extensions slides.
